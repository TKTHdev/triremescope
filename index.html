Rates are commands per second of simulated time. Hot sets that site to 30/s and the rest to 2/s. Click a replica on the map to crash or restart it.

What this models. Weights follow GenWV(N, 1): two thranites of weight 3(N−2), light replicas of weight 2, fast threshold Φ = 3W/4, slow threshold Ψ = W/2. A coordinator sends PreAccept only to FQ(L)∖{L}; matching replies commit on the fast path, conflicts take an Accept round to weight above Ψ, and a silent FQ member triggers the fallback. ADAPT computes ALC(S) = Σ rate(p)·M̂(p,S) for every pair S of unsuspected replicas and proposes RESEAT once the accumulated gap reaches C, S* has held for 3 heartbeats, and the epoch has lasted Tmin. Simplifications. RTTs are illustrative, not measured; map positions are schematic. Clients sit at their replica and fail over to the nearest live one. Conflicts are a coin flip rather than real key overlap, a replica executes a command when its Commit arrives, suspicion uses a fixed 1 s timeout instead of AdaptPing, a restarted replica rejoins at the latest epoch, and every replica sees the same ADAPT ledger. Demo settings: heartbeat 100 ms, Tmin 2 s, θ = 0.05, initial C from a 300 ms fence.
